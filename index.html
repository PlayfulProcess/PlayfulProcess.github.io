About

PlayfulProcess is the pen name of a writer and builder — a former Goldman Sachs equity research analyst turned social-work student, now making open-source tools and open books. The through-line is a single conviction: meaning is something we make together, over time, and the containers that hold that work — families, stories, communities, commons — can be built on purpose.

The books are the argument. recursive.eco is the argument made usable — infrastructure for making sense together with symbolic systems, or grammars: tarot decks, I Ching readings, story sets, interpretation frameworks. The Substack, Life Is Process, is where the thinking happens in public, one essay at a time.
